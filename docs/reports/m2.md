# M2 Report: Design of Settle, Analyze, Fetch and Clean

**Finished:** 2026-10-11. **Awaiting your decisions** (below).

## The project, in two lines

A system that studies the Indian share market each evening and suggests trades for the next one to three days. It never places an order, it runs on fake money first, and it keeps a permanent record of every decision so it can learn from its own results. Full description: [spec.md](../spec.md).

## What M2 was for

To finish the design of the four remaining jobs, so building can start with no open questions:

| Job | What it does | Design |
|---|---|---|
| Settle | Works out what each fake trade would really have done, from daily prices | [pass2-settle.md](../design/pass2-settle.md) |
| Analyze | Picks the 500 shares to watch, reads the market's mood, checks every share against every checklist | [pass3-analyze.md](../design/pass3-analyze.md) |
| Fetch and Clean | Downloads each day's NSE data; checks it before it enters the stored history | [pass4-fetch-clean.md](../design/pass4-fetch-clean.md) |

M2 was design only. The figures below come from 4 years of NSE daily prices and 11 years of index prices, downloaded to test the design. **No profit figures were computed**, so the backtest in M5 remains a fair first test.

## Words used here

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Checklist | A set of yes/no conditions a share must all meet, plus where its stop and target go. |
| Stop / target | The price to sell at if the trade goes wrong / right. |
| Typical daily move | The average size of a share's daily price swing over the last 14 days. |
| Market mood | Up, down or sideways. |
| Unknown | A result daily prices cannot settle, e.g. a day that touched both the stop and the target. |

---

## The design in brief

- **Settle books gaps honestly.** A share that opens below its stop is sold at that open, not at the stop. A day that touched both the stop and the target is "unknown", valued both ways, and never guessed.
- **Clean asks only "did this happen in the market?"** It checks every figure against the previous day, and verifies every split against the actual price move. Anything it cannot verify sits out for a day.
- **Splits are applied to past prices only once they have happened**, so a backtest can never know about a future split.
- **Every check Analyze makes is kept,** matches and misses with their figures, so the record shows what the system saw, not what later code would recompute.
- **Fetch runs at 6pm IST** (NSE publishes at about 4:35pm) and retries until 10pm. A missed day is caught up the next evening.

## What the data showed

| | Quiet breakout | Pullback in an uptrend | Gap up that holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matches per day | about 4 | about 18 | about 0.4 |
| Results that would be unknown | 0.5% | 0.1% | 1.4% |
| Too expensive for the ₹2,000 limit | 25% | 22% | 24% |

- **There will be more candidates than room**: about 22 a day against room for about 2 new trades. Paper trading should produce roughly 100–150 trades, more than M1 estimated.
- **Unknown results will be rare**, under 1.5%.
- **Real risk per trade will usually be about ₹60, not ₹100.** The typical stop is 3.2% below the price, so the ₹2,000 limit per share binds on about 72% of trades. This follows from your limits; no change is needed unless you want one.
- **Market mood, 2015–2026:** up 59% of days, down 22%, sideways 20%. A mood lasts about 15 trading days on average.

## Decisions for you

### 1. Market mood

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Nifty 500 index against its 50-day average (recommended)**: up if above a rising average, down if below a falling one, otherwise sideways | What most people mean by "the market". Easy to check on any chart. | One more NSE file to fetch each day. |
| B. Breadth: the share of our 500 stocks above their own 50-day average | Uses only data we already have. | Harder to picture and to check by eye. |

### 2. The first checklists

| Checklist | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Quiet breakout | The 10-day range is within 8%; today closes above it; volume at least 2× normal. |
| Pullback in an uptrend | Above a rising 50-day average; closed lower on each of the last 3 days. |
| Gap up that holds | Opened at least 3% up; closed at or above the open, in the top quarter of the day's range; volume at least 3× normal. |

All three share the same exits: **stop one typical daily move below the close, target 1.5 typical daily moves above, sell after 3 trading days.**

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **A. All three (recommended)** | Three different kinds of move. The gap-up costs little: thin evidence borrows from the overall record, and M5's backtest drops any checklist that loses. | Gap-ups will produce only about 10 paper trades. |
| B. Drop the gap-up, keep two | More evidence per checklist. | Loses the one pattern driven by news. |

## Calls made by Claude during M2 (logged in [decisions/](../decisions/))

- **Downloads are not kept, only receipts with checksums** (0005). Saves about 50 MB a year; NSE keeps its archive public.
- **The same exits for every checklist** (0006). Differences in results then come down to the entry conditions.
- **Fetch uses IPv4 and short timeouts** (0007). NSE's IPv6 address hung for 30 seconds per request on your network.
