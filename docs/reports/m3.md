# M3 Report: The first working slice

**Finished:** 2026-10-11

## The project, in two lines

A system that studies the Indian share market each evening and suggests trades for the next one to three days. It never places an order, it runs on fake money first, and it keeps a permanent record of every decision so it can learn from its own results. Full description: [spec.md](../spec.md).

## What M3 was for

To build the system's spine as working code and prove it end to end on real past data:

> **fetch** prices → **clean** them → check **one checklist** → **decide** with fixed trade sizes → **record** → **settle**

and to build the test that guards the spec's most important rule: **never look at the future**. Learning, confidence, the other two checklists and Claude's review come in M4.

## Words used here

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Replay | Running the system over past days, one evening at a time, as if live. Written to a practice folder, never to the real record. |
| History | The verified NSE prices stored in the repo (`data/history/`). |
| Don't know | Clean could not verify a share's figures that day, so it sat out. |
| Unknown | A trade result daily prices cannot settle (the day touched both its stop and target). |

## What M3 built

| Part | What it does | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Fetch | Downloads NSE's daily price file, index closes and corporate actions, with a receipt for every request | `src/trader/fetch/` |
| Clean | Reads both NSE file formats; checks every share every day; verifies splits and bonuses against the price; stores the history | `src/trader/clean/` |
| Analyze | Picks the monthly top 500; reads the market mood (Nifty 500 vs its 50 DMA); runs the quiet-breakout checklist; records every check | `src/trader/analyze/` |
| Decide | Proposes buys within your limits (₹10,000, 1% risk, 5 positions, 20% per share, 5% total risk), and time-limit exits | `src/trader/decide/` |
| Record | Writes proposals, skips, decisions and results to the notebook, all linked | `src/trader/record/` |
| Settle | Works out fills, exits, unknowns and the account (worse and better case) from real prices | `src/trader/settle/` |
| Run | `backfill` fetches and cleans past days into the history; `replay` runs the trading jobs over them | `src/trader/run.py` |

**57 automatic tests, all passing**, on your laptop and on GitHub's machines:
- one per row of the Settle and Clean design tables;
- each checklist condition, true and false;
- the original 22 structure rules.

## The lookahead test

Two made-up markets are identical up to a cut-off date and different after it. The system is replayed over both, and every check, proposal and decision made up to the cut-off must come out identical. **It passes.** To prove the test has teeth, a one-day leak was planted on purpose, letting the system see tomorrow's price: the test failed. The leak was then removed.

## Replay over April 2024 to October 2026

Written to a practice folder, not the repo (decision 0009). **No profit figures are reported**, so M5's backtest stays a fair first test.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Trading days replayed | 627 (up 305, sideways 142, down 180) |
| Share checks recorded | 313,500: every share in the universe, every day, matches and misses |
| Quiet-breakout matches | 2,078 (about 3.3 a day) |
| Buys decided | 714. 700 entered, 14 cancelled for opening beyond their exit |
| Every exit route occurred | Stop, target, time limit, gap through the stop, gap through the target, unknown |
| Unknown results | 4 (0.6% of trades) |
| Limit breaches | **None.** Never more than 5 positions; no position over 20% of the balance; no trade risking over 1%; total open risk peaked at 4.2%; cash never negative |
| Links | Every result points to its decision, and every decision to the check that produced it |
| Candidates skipped | 819 for lack of room, 530 unaffordable under the limits, 15 already held |

## Problems found and fixed during M3

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| NSE held a special Saturday session (18 May 2024). Skipping it made every share's next "previous close" look wrong: 1,845 false "don't know"s | Fetch tries every calendar day (decision 0010) |
| ETFs trade alongside shares, and their unit splits looked like 90% crashes | Keep only company shares (ISIN starting INE) |
| A split and a bonus on the same day were checked one at a time, and both failed | Actions taking effect together are checked together |
| CGCL rose exactly 20% (NSE's widest daily limit) on its split day, and was rejected by a rounding hair | Tolerance set to 21%, with a test for this exact case |
| An unverifiable split would have left an unexplained jump in a share's history | The share's earlier history is dropped instead |
| NSE's IPv6 address hung for 30 seconds per request on your network | Fetch connects over IPv4 (decision 0007) |
